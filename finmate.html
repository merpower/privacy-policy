3. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Financial Records & Local Content

All expense entries, incomes, category budgets, bank account aliases, debts/obligations, recurring bill rules, and notes created in FinMate are stored strictly locally on your device via an isolated SQLite database. We do not read, transmit, sell, or host your monetary records on external servers. Furthermore:

  • Zero Bank Scraping: The Application does not connect to Open Banking APIs, scrape banking portals, or ask for your bank account credentials, credit card numbers, or online banking passwords.
  • No Account Required: You can use FinMate immediately without creating an account or providing your name or email.

B. Device Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used strictly for scheduled local notifications to remind you of upcoming bill due dates, recurring expenses, and payday updates. Notifications are generated 100% locally on your device.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used exclusively to trigger bill and budget reminders precisely at the scheduled time.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT): Used to unlock the Application when you enable App Lock. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by your device operating system hardware keystore; FinMate never collects, stores, or accesses your biometric templates. Custom PINs are hashed locally using salted SHA-256 and never leave your device.
  • Storage Access (User-Initiated): Used only when you explicitly choose to export or import local CSV or JSON backup files using the Android Storage Access Framework.
  • No Camera / Microphone / Location: FinMate does not request or use Camera, Microphone, or GPS/Location permissions.

C. Data Portability & Local Backups

You maintain full ownership and control over your financial records. You can export your entire transaction history to standard CSV spreadsheets or JSON backup files directly to your device storage at any time. We do not perform automatic cloud synchronization.

D. Third-Party Services & Diagnostics

To support ongoing maintenance and provide optional features, the Application integrates the following trusted third-party SDKs:

  • Google AdMob & UMP: Used to display advertisements supporting free usage. AdMob and its mediation partners use pseudonymous mobile advertising identifiers (GAID) in accordance with GDPR/ePrivacy regulations. You can manage or revoke consent at any time via Settings > Privacy Options, or upgrade to the Ad-Free version.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics: Collects anonymous diagnostic metrics (device model, OS version, app crashes) to ensure app stability. No financial amounts, account balances, or transaction notes are ever sent to analytics.
  • Google Play Billing & RevenueCat: Handles optional in-app purchases (such as the permanent Ad-Free Pro upgrade). All payment details and card transactions are processed securely and exclusively by Google Play. We never receive or store your financial payment credentials.

4. Data Retention, Export and Deletion

  • In-App Deletion: You can edit or permanently delete individual transactions, categories, and accounts at any time.
  • Complete Database Reset: You can wipe all stored data instantly from within the app via Settings > Database & Storage > Reset Database.
  • Uninstalling: Removing the Application permanently deletes all locally stored databases and settings from your device.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

FinMate does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). The app does not target children.

6. Security of Your Personal Data

Because your financial data resides solely on your physical device, its security is anchored in Android's application sandboxing architecture. We enhance this protection with local biometric locking, salted PIN hashing, and secure TLS/HTTPS protocols for diagnostic telemetry.
