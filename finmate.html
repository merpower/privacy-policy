FinMate is designed with a Privacy-First, 100% Offline philosophy. We believe your personal financial data belongs solely to you. We do not operate cloud servers storing your financial records, transactions, or banking credentials.

1. Introduction

Welcome to FinMate: Expense & Budget ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information Collection and Storage

2.1 100% Local Financial Data Storage

  • All financial records—including account balances, transaction history, expense categories, recurring obligations, and savings goals—are stored strictly locally on your device in a private SQLite database.
  • Zero Cloud Sync of Financial Data: We do not operate remote financial servers or synchronize your personal transactions to any cloud database.
  • No Banking Credentials: The App does not connect to Open Banking APIs, scrape bank websites, or request banking passwords, card numbers, or PINs.

2.2 Biometric & App Lock Data

  • Authentication is handled entirely by your device’s local operating system keystore (local_auth).
  • We never have access to, collect, or transmit your fingerprint, face data, or biometric templates.
  • Custom PINs are hashed locally using salted SHA-256 and never leave your device.

2.3 Automated Data Collection & Third-Party SDKs

Certain third-party libraries may process limited, pseudonymous technical identifiers:

  1. Google Firebase Analytics:
    • Collects aggregated diagnostic events (screen views, OS version, app crashes) to improve stability. Financial figures and note contents are never logged.
    • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  2. Google AdMob & Meta Audience Network:
    • Displays advertisements to support the free version in compliance with GDPR.
    • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  3. RevenueCat (In-App Purchases):
    • Processes in-app purchases (e.g. permanent Ad-Removal) securely via Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your card or bank details.
    • Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

3. Device Permissions

Permission Purpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Used strictly for scheduled local notifications to remind you of upcoming bills and payday alerts. Notifications are generated 100% locally.
USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT Used to verify your identity locally when unlocking the App.
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required exclusively for loading advertisements and anonymous crash/analytics telemetry.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Required on Android 12+ to deliver precise, on-time bill reminder notifications.

4. Data Retention, Export & Deletion

  • Local CSV & JSON Backup: You can export all your transactions to CSV spreadsheets or full JSON backup files at any time.
  • Instant Data Wiping: You can permanently delete all accounts and transactions directly in the App under Settings → Database & Storage → Reset Database.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored application data.

5. Children's Privacy & Security

FinMate does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We apply local sandbox isolation, salted SHA-256 PIN hashing, and TLS/HTTPS network encryption.
