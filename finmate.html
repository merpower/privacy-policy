3. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. User Content (Notes, Checklists, Audio & Images)

All notes, to-do checklists, tags, voice memos, and image attachments created in My Notes are stored locally on your device in a private, sandboxed database. We do not read, transmit, or monetize your personal content on external servers without your explicit action.

B. Device Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used solely when recording voice notes. Audio is stored on-device and never transmitted to third parties.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used exclusively when you choose to scan a QR code for 1-Tap Note Sharing, or when taking a photo to attach to a note.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC): Used to unlock the app when you enable App Lock. Biometric authentication is handled strictly by the Android operating system; My Notes never collects or stores biometric data.
  • Notifications & Alarms: Used to deliver your scheduled reminders.
  • Location: My Notes does not collect, request, or track your physical GPS or location data.

C. Cloud Backups & 1-Tap Note Sharing (User-Initiated)

  • Google Drive Cloud Backup: Backups are stored directly in your own private Google Drive storage via Google Sign-In. We do not have access to your credentials or cloud storage.
  • 1-Tap Note Sharing: If you share a note via 6-digit code or QR, only that specific note is temporarily hosted on our secure cloud backend (Firebase). You can terminate sharing at any time with 1 tap.

D. Third-Party Services & Diagnostics

  • Google AdMob & UMP: To display ads and manage consent in compliance with GDPR and Google policies. You can adjust consent anytime via Settings > "Privacy Options".
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Anonymous diagnostic crash logs to improve app stability.
  • In-App Purchases (RevenueCat): Pro subscriptions are processed securely via Google Play Billing.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control to edit or permanently delete your notes, checklists, and attachments at any time within the app. Uninstalling the application will delete all local data unless backed up.
