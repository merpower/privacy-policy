User Content (Notes, Checklists, Audio & Images) Local Storage First: All notes, to-do checklists, tags, voice memos, and image attachments created in My Notes are stored locally on your device in a private, sandboxed database. No Unauthorized Transmission: We do not read, transmit, or monetize your personal notes on external servers without your explicit action. B. Device Permissions Requested by the App The Application only requests device permissions strictly necessary to provide its core features: Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used solely when you record voice notes. Audio is processed on-device and never transmitted to any third party. Camera (CAMERA): Used exclusively when you choose to scan a QR code for 1-Tap Note Sharing, or when taking a photo to attach to a note. Biometric Sensors (USE_BIOMETRIC): Used to unlock the app when you enable App Lock. Biometric authentication (fingerprint / face) is handled entirely by the Android operating system; the Application never accesses, collects, or stores your biometric data. Notifications & Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver reminders and alarms that you schedule. Calendar (READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR): Used strictly if you choose to sync your scheduled reminders with your device calendar. Note on Location: My Notes does not collect, request, or track your physical GPS or location data. C. Cloud Backups & 1-Tap Note Sharing (User-Initiated) Google Drive Cloud Backup: If you choose to use the Cloud Backup feature, the app connects directly to your personal Google Drive account via Google Sign-In. Backups are stored in your own private Google Drive storage space. We do not have access to your Google credentials or your cloud storage. 1-Tap Note Sharing: If you choose to share a note with a peer using a 6-digit code or QR code, only that specific note's content is temporarily hosted on our secure cloud backend (Firebase). The shared content is automatically or manually removable, and none of your other local notes are accessible. D.
