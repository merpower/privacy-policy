Privacy Policy

World News Reader - TTS & RSS

Developer: Anastasios Meresentzis

Last updated: February 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application World News Reader - TTS & RSS ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

By using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Personal Information

If you choose to sign in via Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication (for cloud sync features), we may collect your email address, display name, and unique user ID. Account creation is optional; you can use the core features of the app without signing in.

b) Automatically Collected Information

We may collect non-personal and diagnostic information automatically through third-party services, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID, Android ID, Firebase installation ID)
  • Device type, model, and operating system version
  • App performance, usage statistics, and crash logs
  • In-app purchase and subscription status

2. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with trusted third-party SDKs to provide essential features, advertising, and analytics:

  • Google AdMob & Meta Audience Network: Used to display advertisements. They may use device identifiers to serve personalized or non-personalized ads based on user consent.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Used to understand app usage patterns and improve performance.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Used to collect crash reports and diagnostics for app stability.
  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore: Used to securely authenticate users and synchronize user preferences/favorites across devices.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to deliver optional breaking news push notifications.
  • RevenueCat: Used to process and manage in-app subscriptions and purchases securely.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and provide cloud sync for the app
  • To analyze usage trends and improve user experience
  • To monitor stability and resolve bugs/crashes
  • To display advertisements and manage premium subscriptions

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent user data. Information is shared only with our trusted service providers (Google, Meta, RevenueCat) as necessary to operate the services mentioned above, or with legal authorities when required by law.

5. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Users have the right to request the complete deletion of their account and all associated data at any time.

Steps to request deletion:

  1. Send an email to anastasiosmeresentzes@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: World News Reader - Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your registered email address or user ID in the email body.

What happens next: Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete your authentication record, saved favorites, and cloud data within 30 days. Anonymous crash logs and aggregated analytics that cannot identify you may be retained for security and debugging purposes.

6. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

7. Security

All data transmitted between the app and external services is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.
